Propose

A model reads each document and proposes candidate facts, each with the sentence it came from.

Adjudicate

Every candidate is judged on its own: kept, retyped to what its evidence actually says, or vetoed.

Verify

Before anything ships, each surviving fact is re-checked against its quote, one by one. The graph below holds only what passed.

Nothing in the explorer is pre-cut. Search, the document index and the relation filters all run over the full build, and the evidence panel quotes the source contract around each fact.
